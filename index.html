The browser

Everything you use Finder for. Faster.

A clean sidebar, a sortable list, a breadcrumb path bar and a Quick Look pane. Search the folder you're in, or the whole Mac, including the text inside files.

Find anything

Spotlight-powered search across your Mac, with results you can open, show in their folder or drag straight into another app.

Preview in place

Quick Look built in. Select a file and see it on the right, no extra window.

All the basics

Rename, duplicate, new folder, copy path, Move to Trash. Back, forward and up, with the shortcuts you already know.

Your places

Recents, your main folders, iCloud Drive and every connected drive, one click away.

Say what you want. guava.zip finds it.

Type in plain English. guava.zip searches, reads and explains your files, then shows the results as rows you can open or drag. Anything that moves or trashes files asks you first, and it never touches anything outside your home folder.

Cleanup

Get your space back.

Built-in tools that show you what's taking room and let you clear it. Files go to the Trash first, so you can put them back.

Free Up Space

App leftovers, caches and logs, old installers, duplicate copies and developer junk, in one scan.

Big & Forgotten

Your largest files, filtered by size and by when you last opened them.

Twins

Exact duplicates, matched by content, not just by name.

Space Map

Click through your folders to see exactly where the gigabytes went.

Uninstall Apps

Remove an app along with the settings and caches it left behind.

Background Items

See what launches when you log in, and remove the ones you don't want.

System Files

See what's in System & other, like iPhone backups and Xcode files, and clear the safe parts.

Keep

Mark the files you want to keep and guava.zip never suggests removing them.

Trash

See what's in the Trash and empty it when you're sure.
