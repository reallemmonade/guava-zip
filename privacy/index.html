Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

guava.zip is a Mac app made by Guava Ventures ("we"). This policy covers the guava.zip app and the guava.zip website.

The app

guava.zip works on your Mac. It has no accounts and no analytics, and we do not receive your files, your file names or how you use the app.

AI features

guava.zip's AI features are optional and you choose how it runs:

  • Claude (cloud): your request, the names and paths of files the assistant looks at, and the text of files it reads are sent directly from your Mac to Anthropic using your own API key. Anthropic's handling of that data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your API account. We never see it. You can stop the assistant reading file contents in Settings.
  • Ollama (local): everything stays on your Mac.
  • Off: the Ask bar runs a local Spotlight search.

Your API key is stored in a private file on your Mac and is only sent to Anthropic.

Permissions

guava.zip asks macOS for access to your folders (and optionally Full Disk Access) so it can show and clean your files. That access is used only on your Mac. The optional junk check runs in the background on your Mac and never sends anything.

The website

guava.zip is a static site hosted by Cloudflare. We don't use cookies or analytics. Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as IP address) to serve and protect the site; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and keep it only as long as we need it to help you.

Children

guava.zip is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll post changes on this page. Questions: support@guava.ventures.
